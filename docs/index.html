04 / WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY

A model with its limits attached.

Fit, then freeze.

For each asset, centered ordinary least squares fits r = α + Bf + ε on days 1-120. Two correlated fictional factors, Broad and Tilt, are not economic market factors. The intercept is fitted but does not enter covariance. No expected return is estimated for allocation.

The diagonal is a choice.

F and residual Ψ use centered sample covariance with denominator 119. This is accounting, not the unbiased regression error estimator RSS/(n−3). Full Ψ and unshrunk F reconstruct the earlier sample covariance because fitted residuals are orthogonal to fitted factors. Diagonal Ψ discards residual co-movement.

Contributions are signed.

σ² = wTΣw. Asset contribution is wᵢ(Σw)ᵢ/σ. Factor contribution is xₖ(Fx)ₖ/σ, where x = BTw. Residual contribution is wᵢ(Ψw)ᵢ/σ. In full residual mode even a residual contribution can be negative. At zero risk, all Euler contributions and shares are undefined.

No best portfolio.

Holdings are a linear book against reference capital. Net and gross are reported, never normalized. Shorts, leverage, cash yield, financing, costs, tail losses, execution and constraints are not modeled. No optimization, advice, real prices, uploads, accounts, broker connection, storage or trading.
